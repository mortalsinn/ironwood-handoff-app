    How to use the Handoff App

    1. How the Blueprint Works

    Check off the job's components on the Left Panel (stairs, railing type, spindles, glass, welded metal). The Right Panel generates the matching production workflow sections — the exact task chains, order, and QA gates the team agreed on — for each type of work. Tasks are color-coded by phase: setup, fabrication, QA, install, wrap-up. Every job automatically ends with the Accounting Close Out (Jeffery / Zoho Books).

    2. Who Owns What

    • Automatic owners: Tasks pre-assign to the team's default leads from the live roster — you'll see their initials chip on each task. If a default lead ever leaves the roster, their tasks simply come up unassigned for you to pick.
    • The Sales section is intentionally blank — use its "Assign User" button to pick the salesperson. Generating is blocked until every section has an owner.
    • Overrides: "Assign User" on a section header sets that whole section; the small person icon on any single task overrides just that task.
    • Project Owner (top of Scope of Work) optionally sets who owns the whole project in Zoho Projects — separate from task assignees. Leave unset for the default.

    3. Timelines (the "d" boxes)

    • Every task shows its working days in a small "d" box — click and change the number for this job. Section headers show the live day total.
    • On Generate, the app builds a real schedule: tasks run back-to-back (weekends skipped) starting today, with stair and railing work as parallel tracks. The dates land on every task in Zoho Projects — check the Gantt view.
    • Sections are chained in Zoho (finish-to-start), so each crew's work shows "waiting on" the previous section. Dates can still be dragged around in Zoho Projects afterward.

    4. Tailoring a Job

    • Rename any task by clicking its text (your rename sticks, even when toggling options). Delete tasks with the ×, or whole sections with the trash can. Drag tasks to reorder or move them between sections.
    • Custom tasks: "Click to add custom task..." inside any section. Custom sections: "Click to add custom item..." on the Left Panel.
    • Needs Site Measure? (Construction Grade stairs) adds the Joel/Tom measure task only when checked.
    • Wood Components Add-On injects wood ordering, lathe work, and cap rail install — for welded jobs capped with wood.
    • Supply Only scope hides all site work (measures, installs) and adds a Customer Pickup section instead.

    5. Travel / ESA

    The Travel button (top left) pulls the job address from the Deal, auto-calculates one-way KMs, and flags ESA automatically past 50 KM. Saving adds a travel logistics note and task, and prefixes site tasks with [ESA] so crews see it.

    6. Production Notes & @Mentions

    Write notes at the bottom of the Left Panel — type @ plus a name to tag a teammate (they get a CRM notification). Use "Add Another Note" to create separate CRM notes. Notes are pushed to the Deal only after a successful handoff, so retries never duplicate them.

    7. Mark Closed Won

    Updates the Deal's stage, amount, and closing date in CRM. The Deal page behind the widget can't auto-refresh — hit F5 to see the new stage. If you click Generate on a deal that isn't Closed Won yet, this popup appears automatically and continues the handoff after saving. You also get a one-time heads-up the moment you start drafting on a deal that isn't closed.

    8. Generate Production Run

    • Creates the Zoho Project (from the production template), invites every assigned person, and builds all task lists, tasks, owners, dates, and section dependencies.
    • Safe to re-run: generating again on the same Deal re-uses the existing project and only fills in missing pieces — no duplicates.
    • "Handoff Incomplete" means the project exists but some tasks failed — it lists exactly which; click Generate again to retry just those.
    • Your work auto-saves locally as you type, and Save Draft shares it to the Deal itself — anyone opening this widget on the same Deal picks up right where you left off (a banner shows who saved it last). Reset clears the draft for everyone on the deal.
